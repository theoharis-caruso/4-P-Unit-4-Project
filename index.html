Christmas in NYC

Dyker Heights Christmas Lights

Dyker Heights is the best place to see Christmas Lights. This enchanting stroll takes you through the streets, where homeowners decorate for the holidays in truly spectacular fashion. Coming here will surely get you into the holiday spirit!



Macys holiday market

One of the newer holiday destinations in Midtown Manhattan, Macy's Holiday Square offers a wide range of handcrafted gifts, artisanal products and festive treats across three city blocks. This open air market by Urbanspace is a must-see destination in the heart of New York City, and features a multitude of vendors from New York and beyond.
Some of the best things to get at the Holiday Market are:

  • Birria Tacos
  • Hot Chocolates
  • Cheese wheel pasta


Rockefeller Center

The holidays in NYC begin at Rockefeller Center. Whether it’s a visit to the iconic Christmas Tree, a skate on the ice at The Rink, cozying up in the Après Skate Chalets, or taking in the skyline from Top of the Rock, there’s holiday magic in every corner. It’s a holiday tradition you’ll want to make your own.
